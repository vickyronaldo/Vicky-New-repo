# T-1005

## Summary
Teams audio is dead on three machines in the same meeting room.

## Impact
Three machines in the same meeting room are affected; number of users impacted at any time is to-verify; business urgency is potentially high because meeting room calls and collaboration may be disrupted for multiple staff.

## Known facts
- The issue affects Teams audio.
- Three machines are impacted.
- The machines are in the same meeting room.

## Missing information to gather
- Whether the issue affects only this meeting room or other rooms as well to-verify.
- Whether the problem is with speakers, microphones, or both to-verify.
- Whether audio works outside Teams on the affected machines to-verify.
- Whether the same external peripherals, dock, display, or room audio equipment are connected to all three machines to-verify.
- Whether the issue occurs in all Teams meetings or one specific meeting to-verify.
- Whether Teams, Windows, audio drivers, or room equipment changed recently to-verify.
- Whether users are selecting the correct Teams audio device and whether the expected device is visible to-verify.

## Likely catagory
Teams / audio hardware / room system issue.

## First diagnostic step
Check one affected machine first to confirm whether Windows audio works outside Teams and whether the correct input/output device is selected in Teams, then compare the shared room equipment connection used by all three machines.
