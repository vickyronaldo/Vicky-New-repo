# T-1006

## Summary
User reports “everything is slow” after upgrading to Win11 two days ago.

## Impact
Single user reported so far to-verify; issue may affect all device activity rather than one application; business urgency to-verify, but potentially high if normal work across multiple systems is degraded.

## Known facts
- The user upgraded to Win11 two days ago.
- Performance is slow across the device.
- The issue started after the upgrade.

## Missing information to gather
- Whether the slowness is constant or mainly affects startup, sign-in, specific apps, or network tasks to-verify.
- Whether the issue occurs on battery, mains power, or both to-verify.
- Whether CPU, memory, disk, or network usage is high during the slowdown to-verify.
- Whether Windows Update, indexing, OneDrive sync, antivirus scanning, or post-upgrade background tasks are still running to-verify.
- Whether there is low free disk space or limited available memory to-verify.
- Whether the device is overheating, unusually noisy, or showing battery/power issues to-verify.
- Whether other users on similar upgraded devices are reporting the same issue to-verify.

## Likely catagory
Windows performance / post-upgrade issue.

## First diagnostic step
Check Task Manager on the affected device to identify whether CPU, memory, disk, or background processes are driving the slowdown following the Win11 upgrade.
