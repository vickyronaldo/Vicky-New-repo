# Finance Team Folder Access: Self-Help Guide

If you cannot open the Finance team folder, do not worry. Your files are safe, and this problem is usually temporary.

## What You Can Try

1. Check that your internet is working by opening a normal website.
What success looks like: The website opens normally.

2. Close all folder windows.
What success looks like: No folder windows are open.

3. Open a folder window again and try the Finance team folder one more time.
What success looks like: The folder opens and your files are visible.

4. Sign out of Windows, then sign back in.
What success looks like: You can open the Finance team folder after signing back in.

5. If it still fails, wait 2 minutes and try again.
What success looks like: The Finance team folder opens when you try again.

6. Restart your computer and try once more.
What success looks like: The Finance team folder opens after restart.

## If It Still Does Not Work
Contact the IT help team if you still cannot open the Finance team folder after Step 6.

## Details to Include in Your Ticket

Please include:
- Your full name and work login name.
- Your team: Finance.
- The name of the Finance folder you tried to open.
- The exact error message shown on screen.
- The time the issue started.
- Whether this happens every time or only sometimes.
- Whether other Finance colleagues have the same issue.
- Whether you are in the office or working remotely.
- A photo of the error message on your screen, if possible.

Thank you. We know this is disruptive, and we will help restore access as quickly as possible.
