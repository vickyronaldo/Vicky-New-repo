# Sign-In Help You Can Do Yourself

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If you cannot log in, follow these steps in order. You can do all of them safely on your own.

Your files are safe. A log-in problem does not remove your documents.

## What You Can Do Now

1. Wait 2 minutes before trying again.  
This gives the system time to clear a temporary lock.

2. Check your keyboard settings.  
Make sure the ALL CAPS key is off and the typing language is your usual one.

3. Type your password slowly and try one time.  
Do not try many times in a row.

4. If it still does not work, restart your computer.  
After it starts again, try to log in one time only.

5. If log in works, open your email and one team folder to check everything is back to normal.  
If both open, continue your work.

6. If log in still does not work, wait 10 minutes and try one final time.  
Do not keep trying again and again.

## Your Files Are Safe

Your files stay safe even when log in fails. This is a sign-in problem, not a file loss problem.

## Contact the IT Help Desk If...

Contact the IT Help Desk if you still cannot log in after the steps above, or if you see a message saying your work account is locked.

## Details to Add in Your Support Request

Please include:
- Your work ID (the name you use to log in)
- Computer name (shown on the log-in screen or on a sticker)
- Where you are working (office or home)
- The exact time the problem started
- The exact message shown on screen (copy the words exactly)
- Which steps from this guide you already tried
- Whether email opens on your phone or another computer
- Best phone number to reach you
