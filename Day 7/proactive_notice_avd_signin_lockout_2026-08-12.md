Subject: Proactive Update: AVD Sign-In and Account Lockouts

We are aware of an increase in AVD sign-in and account lockout reports this morning. This is a known issue, and our support and engineering teams are actively working on it.

As an interim workaround, please try these steps:
1. Close the AVD app or browser window fully, reopen it, and sign in once using your full work email and current password.
2. If you see “account locked,” wait 15 minutes without any further sign-in attempts, then try one more time.

If you are still unable to sign in, please contact the Service Desk and include: your username, exact error message, and the time of your latest failed attempt.

Please stay calm. We understand this is disruptive and we are treating it as a priority. We will share the next status update within 60 minutes, even if full resolution is still in progress.
