# Win11 Migration Feedback - Top 3 Priorities (Day 7)

Date: 2026-08-12
Source: 50 post-migration Finbridge staff comments
Method: Ranked by combined impact (Blocker > Friction > Minor > Positive) and volume (count), with impact weighted more heavily than volume.

## 1) AVD sign-in and account lockouts (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #1:
- Highest-volume Blocker theme.
- Prevents users from accessing their desktop/work environment at all.
- Repeated lockouts indicate recurring instability rather than one-off incidents.

Manager sentence:
- "Our top risk today is AVD sign-in and lockout failures (7 reports) because affected users are fully unable to work until access is restored."

## 2) Missing files and OneDrive sync/file visibility issues (Count: 5, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #2:
- Blocker-level data access issue with urgent business deadlines called out.
- Slightly lower volume than AVD lockouts, but still high and time-critical.
- Directly impacts report delivery and confidence in migrated data integrity.

Manager sentence:
- "OneDrive missing-file incidents (5 reports) are our second priority because users cannot access critical documents needed for same-day deliverables."

## 3) Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance) (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #3:
- Lower volume than several Friction themes, but Blocker impact outweighs those counts.
- Affected users are unable to complete core finance tasks (for example month-end reporting).
- Scope appears concentrated but business-critical for impacted teams.

Manager sentence:
- "Shared drive access denial (3 reports) is third priority since each case is a hard blocker to finance operations despite lower overall volume."

---

Note:
- High-volume Friction themes (for example Floor 3 printer mapping and slow login) remain important, but are ranked below lower-volume Blockers due to immediate work stoppage risk.
