## AVD Sign-In or Account Locked
1. Close the AVD app/browser window completely, reopen it, and sign in once using your full work email and current password.
2. If it says locked, wait 15 minutes without more sign-in attempts, then try one more time.

If you still cannot sign in or still see “account locked,” contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact error message, time of latest failed attempt, and whether this happened before.

## OneDrive Files Missing
1. Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and click “Resume syncing” if paused, then wait 5-10 minutes and check your files again.
2. Open OneDrive on the web (office.com) and check if the missing file is there, including Recent and Recycle Bin.

If files are still missing, contact the Service Desk and share: file/folder names, last time you saw them, where you checked (desktop/web), and any OneDrive error message shown.

## Shared Drive Access Denied (S: / Finance Drive)
1. Disconnect and reconnect VPN, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. Restart your laptop once and retry opening the drive from File Explorer.

If access is still denied, contact the Service Desk and share: drive letter/path (for example S:), exact error text, your team/department, and whether colleagues can access it.
