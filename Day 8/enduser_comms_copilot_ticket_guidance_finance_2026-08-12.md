# End-User Communication: Copilot Ticket Guidance (Finance)

Date: 2026-08-12  
Audience: Finance users and support requesters  
Purpose: Explain common Copilot issues in plain English and what to do next.

## Before You Start

Most Copilot issues are caused by access permissions, policy controls, or setup timing, not a Copilot product defect. Please follow the steps below for your scenario.

---

## 1) "Copilot won't summarise the Q3 board pack in SharePoint"

What this means in plain English:
- You can see the file, but Copilot may not have the same usable access path to read and summarize it in context.
- Sensitive board content may also be restricted by policy.

What to do next:
1. Open the board pack directly from SharePoint (not from an old or forwarded link).
2. Confirm you can view the file content fully, not just the folder.
3. Try again from the same signed-in work account.
4. If it still fails, raise a support ticket with the file link and exact error text.

---

## 2) "New hire: Copilot in Outlook knows nothing about my recent emails"

What this means in plain English:
- New accounts often need time for mailbox and content indexing before Copilot can use recent information.

What to do next:
1. Confirm you are signed in with your company account in Outlook.
2. Check with IT that your Copilot license is assigned.
3. Wait a short period and retry (new starters can see delays early on).
4. If still unchanged, share screenshots and timestamps with support.

---

## 3) "I asked Copilot to use a sensitive salary spreadsheet and got 'I don't have access'"

What this means in plain English:
- Copilot is respecting security boundaries. If you cannot access that content with your current permissions, Copilot cannot use it either.

What to do next:
1. Try opening the spreadsheet directly with your own account.
2. If access is denied, request access through your normal approval route.
3. If you believe you should already have access, contact the file owner or Finance IT.
4. Retry Copilot only after access is confirmed.

---

## 4) "Copilot in Teams can't find a contract shared via guest link from another organization"

What this means in plain English:
- External guest-shared content from another tenant may not be available in the same way for Copilot grounding.

What to do next:
1. Open the contract directly from the shared guest link first.
2. Confirm whether the file is hosted outside our organization.
3. Ask the owner for an approved internal copy if needed for business use.
4. If business critical, log a ticket and include the tenant/source details.

---

## 5) "Copilot stopped working for the whole Finance team this morning"

What this means in plain English:
- A team-wide sudden outage usually points to licensing, service health, or tenant configuration changes.

What to do next:
1. Do a quick sign-out/sign-in once.
2. Check whether teammates have the same issue.
3. Report to IT Service Desk immediately as a team-wide incident.
4. Include when it started and which apps are affected (Outlook, Teams, Word, etc.).

---

## 6) "Copilot summarized a file I forgot I had access to"

What this means in plain English:
- Copilot can use content you are allowed to access, even if you forgot that permission existed.
- This is usually a permissions hygiene issue, not Copilot breaking security.

What to do next:
1. Stop and do not share the output further.
2. Report the file path to Finance IT/security for access review.
3. Ask for least-privilege cleanup if the access is no longer needed.
4. Re-run only after permissions are corrected.

---

## 7) "Copilot gives generic answers and ignores internal SharePoint content"

What this means in plain English:
- Copilot may not be fully enabled for your account/client yet, or it cannot reach the content due to access/setup constraints.

What to do next:
1. Confirm Copilot is licensed on your account.
2. Update Office and Teams to current supported versions.
3. Test with a known file you can definitely open directly.
4. If still generic, raise a ticket with the prompt you used and expected source file.

---

## 8) "Copilot in Outlook can't see a shared mailbox calendar I manage for my director"

What this means in plain English:
- Delegated or shared mailbox access does not always behave the same as your own mailbox context.

What to do next:
1. Confirm you can open the shared calendar directly in Outlook.
2. Verify delegated permissions are still active.
3. Retry from the same signed-in account and client.
4. If still blocked, log a support ticket with mailbox/delegation details.

---

## What to Include in Any Copilot Support Ticket

To speed up resolution, include:
- The app used (Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Exact error message text
- Time the issue occurred
- File/mailbox/site link involved
- Screenshot of the issue
- Whether colleagues can reproduce it

## Reassurance

Copilot follows your existing Microsoft 365 access permissions and policy controls. If access is corrected and setup prerequisites are met, most issues are resolved without product escalation.
