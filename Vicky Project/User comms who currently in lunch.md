# Floor 6 Service Update for Partners (Lunch Brief)

Date: 2026-08-14
Audience: Partners and business leads

## What happened
This morning, a large number of people on Floor 6 had problems signing in, with some unable to log in and others seeing very slow access.

At the same time, we also received reports of missing desktop shortcuts for some users.

We have linked these issues to a recent app rollout that was targeted to this group.

## What we are doing right now
We have paused and rolled back the rollout for the affected Floor 6 group.

We are checking affected devices, one by one, to confirm that sign-in and desktop access are returning to normal.

We are prioritising users who are currently blocked from work.

Our service desk is tracking every affected user so no one is missed.

## What is still open
Not all users are fully recovered yet.

We are still confirming exact user impact numbers and making sure the issue does not return after rollback.

We are also reviewing all related reports to make sure there are no separate issues hidden inside this incident.

## What partners can expect next
You will get another clear status update after the current validation checks complete.

That next update will include:
- how many users are fully recovered
- who still needs support
- what follow-up actions are being taken to prevent repeat impact

## What we need from partners now
Please ask any Floor 6 user still affected to raise or update a service desk ticket with:
- their name
- device name
- time they last tried to sign in

This helps us prioritise and resolve remaining cases faster.
